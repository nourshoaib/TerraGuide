Crop Advisor

How It Works

Our system analyzes multiple scientific parameters to provide optimal crop suggestions:

  1. Soil Chemistry: N, P, K levels and pH balance
  2. Climate Factors: Current temperature and humidity
  3. Water Availability: Expected rainfall in your region
  4. Location Data: Local weather patterns for your area

Ideal Input Ranges:

  • N (Nitrogen): 0-200 ppm
  • P (Phosphorus): 0-200 ppm
  • K (Potassium): 0-200 ppm
  • pH: 0-14 scale
  • Rainfall: 0-300mm/month
